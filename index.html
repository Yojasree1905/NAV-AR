Voice Commands

Speak naturally using the "Hey Nav" wake phrase, or tap the mic:

"Hey Nav, take me to [place]" Routes to any named building or place
"Hey Nav, repeat" Replays last guidance
"Hey Nav, where am I" Reports GPS position & distance
"Hey Nav, stop" Cancels current route
"Hey Nav, help" Spoken list of commands
"Hey Nav, what's ahead" AI describes current scene (needs OpenAI key)
